# Gemma 4 E4B on the global suite (2026-11-22)

Model: `gemma-4-e4b-it` (Gemma 4 E4B, a small open-weight model), served on a Kaggle T4 GPU. Suite: global, 6 tasks per
category, 30 tasks; ground truth computed by hijridate, pyluach and phonenumbers. Same 4,096-token output budget and
one-time "answer now" retry rule in both conditions as the published 26B run.

its 16 differential cases are recorded in the skill's `provenance.json`. There is no Jewish-holiday skill in `skills/`
yet, so that category ran without one in both conditions.
